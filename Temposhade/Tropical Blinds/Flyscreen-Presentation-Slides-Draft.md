## Slide 3: Recommended Range Structure

**Title**
Recommended Starting Range

**Bullets**
- Shutters: top-down blackout, top-down blackout plus flyscreen, framed sliding option, and value freehanging option.
- Large openings: Sliderscreen PLUS as the primary solution for bi-fold and patio areas.
- General windows and doors: Sliderscreen PRO as the main flexible mid-range product.

**Key message**

## Slide 4: Shutters

**Title**
Shutter Use Case

**Bullets**
- Main requirement: blackout blind within existing shutter frames, ideally fitting the smaller 90mm frame as well as the 100mm frame.
- Critical issue: stacking height must stay visually clean when shutter louvres are open.
- Best product types for this use case:
- Top Down Cord Tensioned, Single Fabric
- Top Down Cord Tension, Double Fabric
- Sliderscreen MINI
- Cordless Freehanging

**Callout**
Target benefit: give Tropical both premium and practical options for new and existing shutter installations.

**Visual suggestion**
Simple frame diagram showing 90mm and 100mm frame depth constraints.

Double Fabric

**Card 3**

- Framed sliding option for shutter contexts
- Depth no greater than 90mm
- Suitable for new installs and some existing 46mm L-frame installs

- Simple, lower-cost option

- Max width 1.6m; max length 1.6m

## Slide 6: Large Openings

**Title**
Large Bi-Fold and Patio Openings

**Subtitle**
Primary recommendation: Sliderscreen PLUS

**Bullets**
- Best fit for wide openings where stability is the priority.
- V-Screen orientation.
- Fabric options:
- 20mm flyscreen mesh in black
- 20mm honeycomb in light filtering, blockout, and sheer white voile
- Max size: 5900mm x 2500mm
- Intermediate rails are required when panel width exceeds half the drop.
- Bottom track retracts into the side channel, leaving the opening largely clear.

**Callout**
This is the strongest recommendation for patio doors and bi-fold areas because it handles scale without becoming unstable.

---

## Slide 7: General Windows and Doors

**Title**
General Windows and Doors

**Subtitle**
Primary recommendation: Sliderscreen PRO

**Bullets**
- Most flexible all-round option for side windows and doors.
- Available in both V-Screen and H-Screen formats.
- Fabric options:
- 25mm flyscreen mesh in black or white
- 25mm honeycomb in light filtering, blockout, and flyscreen
- Max sizes:
- V-Screen: 2800mm x 2200mm
- H-Screen: 1800mm x 1600mm, double frame only
- Double frame is recommended for doors to reduce bowing and flex.
- Single frame is best limited to simpler V-Screen window applications.

**Callout**
If Tropical wants one core general-purpose framed screen product, this is the most sensible choice.

---

## Slide 8: Conservatories and Secondary Window Use

**Title**
Conservatories and Secondary Window Use

**Subtitle**
Primary recommendation: Sliderscreen LITE

**Bullets**
- Recommended for conservatory windows and lighter-duty side window applications.
- Can also be fitted behind a shutter frame before shutter installation.
- Available in V-Screen and H-Screen formats.
- Fabric options:
- 20mm flyscreen mesh in black
- 20mm honeycomb in light filtering, blockout, and sheer white voile
- Max sizes:
- V-Screen: 2800mm x 1500mm
- H-Screen: 1500mm x 2000mm
- Not recommended as the main choice for doors.

**Callout**
This gives Tropical a simpler conservatory and side-window offer without over-specifying the frame.
