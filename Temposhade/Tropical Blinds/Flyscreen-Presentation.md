This page is providing my initial selection of products and thoughts regarding Tropical's requirements

Primarily Tropical requires a fly screen product for windows and doors, as well as a product that can be used as a sliding blackout. The Sliderscreen MINI will be ideal for this.

Following on from that they also require a product which could be used as a blackout blind but also as a fly screen. The following profiles exist in the Tropical range already and they are looking for something that would fit this. We could give them a framed product but that would make installation possibly a little bit more difficult because a framed product would need to be built with the shutter frame itself. Alternatively we could provide a top fix product which would make use of the existing side channels to cover the edges of the fabric.

SHUTTERS
For plantation shutters the customer is likely to order a blackout blind to fit within the existing frames that Tropical do. The smaller 90mm frame has a gap of 28.7mm; the larger 100mm frame has a gap of 39.1mm. Ideally a blind should be able to fit inside the 90mm frame to give customers more options for installation especially where space is reduced in a recess. The options would be: 
- a blackout blind on its own
- a blackout blind with an integrated fly screen
 For this use case we could introduce three products: 
1. Top Down Cord Tensioned - Single Fabric - This is a standard top-down pleated blind which is fixed by a top-fix bracket and anchor points at the bottom of the blind. We would suggest that the maximum size that one should try to sell is 1500mm wide. This is because the blind relies on tension and blinds of wider widths will produce significant weight over time. Tension could be lost and need re-tightening. Tightening could be done using the feet at the bottom however (internal note: we should probably push the factory to use a spring on this blind to ensure less friction during up-down movements).
	1. This blind will work because the headrail at the top is very slim and the fabric is 25 mm, producing a small stacking height. This is especially useful behind shutters because blinds which have a large stacking height can, in some cases, stack to a deeper size than the actual top bar of the shutter panel. This can be unsightly when louvres are open
	2. Profile Colours are available in black and white
	3. A no-screw accessory is available which has self-adhesive pads for side fixing; however this may be adaptable to face fixing in the future. Currently the adhesive pad, which is strong and suitable, only comes in white. Meaning that for now the product should only be used with white profiles and fitting into white openings or within the shutter frame
2. Top Down Cord Tension - Double Fabric - The profiles are an operation method exactly the same as the top-down cell tension; however we add a second fabric, which is a fly screen mesh. The fly screen mesh sits at the bottom of the blind since the weight of the mesh is less than the blackout fabric at the top.
3. Sliderscreen MINI - This product is a framed blind with a 5mm flange overlap. Overall it's no deeper than 90mm and is ideal for fitting into new shutter installations (behind the frame, but attached to the frame). 
   It will also fit into existing shutter installations (within a standard 46mm L-Frame) provided that the height of the stacked blind, does not exceed the height of the shutter panel top rail. 
4. Cordless Freehanging - Cordless freehanging is worth considering, given that the shutter profile already has what is effectively a side channel to provide guidance to the blind as it comes down. It may be that a small rod is needed to hook the profiles on windows which are out of height; however, this product is simple to fit and low cost
	1. Maximum width: 1.6m
	2. Maximum length: 1.6m

Flyscreens for Windows and Doors

finding a suitable suite of products to offer as flyscreens or even cellular blind coverings is difficult. Categories can be broken down into three: 
1. Large bi-fold doors and patio areas
2. Side windows 
3. Conservatory rooms typically made of PVC or aluminium
we can cover all three categories using the range of products that we have available to us. 


Product Orientation/Direction Definition

	- Blind moves up/down
- V-Screen
	- Blind moves side/side

Max Dimension: 5900mm x 2500mm

For large bi-fold doors and patio areas we would use the Sliderscreen PLUS. We will typically use a 20 mm wide fabric. It has intermediate rails, which must be inserted into the fabric panel whenever the panel width would exceed half of the drop. For example a window of 3 m wide by 2 m drop would require two panels. that's because if we divide 3 m wide by 2 panels, we would have 2 panels at 1.5 m wide each. The guiding fabric chain that sits at the bottom of the blind can retract up into the side channel of the panel easily since it is less than the height of the profile itself

The Sliderscreen PLUS can go to 5.9 m wide. There would be a join in the headbox over 2.95mt wide, which isn't noticeable.

the main feature of this product is that it offers extreme stability over large areas. The fabric is supported at the bottom of the blind by a track which, when open, retracts up into the side channel, leaving the opening almost completely clear at the bottom of the window. The only requirement is for a 3 mm high self-adhesive door bar upon which the track will run.

For smaller doors or in situations where the customer doesn't want panels or indeed they just need a lower price point, the Sliderscreen Pro can accommodate their needs. this product doesn't have any intermediate rails. The fabric stacks in one piece from left to right or of course it could be split draw if required. 
this product has two specifications: 
1. one with a double frame
2. one without

Double Frame 
the double frame version is used for reinforcing side channels and top channels. This type of product will suffer from bowing and flexing under the strain of tension and weight of fabric if it's not reinforced. The double frame also provides a face fixing or side fixing point
for door installations it is highly recommended to use a double frame on every installation.
the floor bar or door threshold is a curved front aluminium profile (12mm high). 

Single Frame
the single frame version is essentially just the actual framed blind without the double frame reinforcement. This is useful on normal windows where a floor bar is not required and the height does not exceed 1.5 m. With that said most installers would rather fit with a double frame.
